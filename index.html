YouTube DJ

A two-deck DJ app for the Mac

YouTube DJ is a small personal project: a Mac app with two turntables and a mixer for mixing YouTube tracks. Paste a link into each deck, or open a playlist or radio, and mix with tempo sync, EQ, filters, loops and beat-matched automatic transitions.

Your YouTube account (optional). To open your own private playlists and liked music, YouTube DJ can sign in with Google. It asks only for permission to view your YouTube account, uses that only to list your playlists and likes inside the app, and keeps everything on your Mac. See the privacy policy for details.

YouTube DJ is shared privately with friends and isn't available for public download. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
